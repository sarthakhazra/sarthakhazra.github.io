02 / Algebraic & Enumerative Geometry

Research

MS Thesis • IISER Mohali

Degrees of Loci in Projective Constructions and their Degenerations

Supervised by Dr. Chetan Tukaram Balwe

Many enumerative questions ask for the degree of a locus—a variety cut out by a geometric condition inside a parameter space. When the initial condition data reaches a degenerate or highly symmetric state, degree counts drops. My thesis investigates the algebraic mechanisms behind these degree-drop phenomena using the machinery of William Fulton’s Intersection Theory

Scheme Theory • IISER Tirupati

Introduction to Schemes

Advisor: Prof. D. S. Nagaraj

Studied sheaves, the prime spectrum $\mathrm{Spec}\,A$ with the Zariski topology, morphisms of schemes, quasi-coherent sheaves of modules, and divisors following Robin Hartshorne’s Algebraic Geometry (Chapter II).

Classical Varieties • IISER Tirupati

Basic Algebraic Geometry

Advisor: Dr. Girja Shanker Tripathi

Worked through Hilbert's Nullstellensatz, regular maps, dimension theory, ringed space structures on varieties, tangent spaces, tangent cones, and local singularities following J. S. Milne's notes.

Other Guided Projects: Also completed reading projects in de Rham Cohomology & Characteristic Classes (with Dr. Subhash B) and Representation Theory of Finite Groups (with Prof. D. S. Nagaraj).
