Research

Research Interests:-

My research interest lies in Algebraic Geometry. More specifically, I am interested in Enumerative Geometry and Intersection Theory problems.

Current project

I am working under the supervision of Dr. Chetan Tukaram Balwe. My thesis project focuses on Enumerative Geometry, specifically exploring the degrees of loci in projective constructions and their degenerations. As part of this project, I am currently studying intersection theory from Fulton’s Intersection Theory, focusing on the foundations of cycles, Chow ring, Cartier divisors, intersection products, and the construction of Chern and Segre classes.

My broader research interests lie in Algebraic Geometry, Algebraic Topology, and Representation Theory. More specifically, I am interested in Enumerative Geometry and Intersection theory.
