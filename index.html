01 / Research & Experience

MS Thesis & Guided Projects

MS Thesis • June 2026 – Present

Degrees of Loci in Projective Constructions and their Degenerations

Advisor: Dr. Chetan Tukaram Balwe (IISER Mohali)

Investigating the algebraic mechanisms underlying degree-drop phenomena and developing a framework for tracking the degrees of loci generated through sequences of projective constructions and their degenerations. Working through the foundations of algebraic cycles, rational equivalence, the Chow ring $\text{CH}^*(X)$, Cartier divisors, intersection products, and Chern and Segre classes.

Primary Reference: Intersection Theory by William Fulton.
Semester Project • Jan – Apr 2026

Introduction to Schemes

Advisor: Prof. D. S. Nagaraj (IISER Tirupati)

Studied sheaves and morphisms of sheaves, construction of the prime spectrum $\text{Spec } A$ with the Zariski topology, definitions and properties of schemes, morphisms of schemes, sheaves of modules, and divisors.

Reference: Algebraic Geometry (Chapter II) by Robin Hartshorne.
Semester Project • Aug – Dec 2025

Algebraic Geometry

Advisor: Dr. Girja Shanker Tripathi (IISER Tirupati)

Explored algebraic sets, Hilbert's Nullstellensatz, the Zariski topology, regular functions and maps, dimension theory, the ringed space structure of affine algebraic varieties, and the local study of tangent spaces, tangent cones, singularities, and regularity.

Reference: J. S. Milne's Algebraic Geometry lecture notes.
Summer Project • May – Jul 2025

de Rham Cohomology and Characteristic Classes

Advisor: Dr. Subhash B (IISER Tirupati)

Focused on exterior algebra, differential forms, chain complexes, de Rham cohomology, Mayer–Vietoris sequences, homotopy invariance, smooth manifolds, and integration of differential forms to translate analytical problems into cohomological formulations.

Reference: From Calculus to Cohomology by Ib Madsen and Jørgen Tornehave.
Semester Reading Project • Dec 2024 – Apr 2025

Representation Theory of Finite Groups

Advisor: Prof. D. S. Nagaraj (IISER Tirupati)

Studied group representations, Maschke's theorem, modules over group algebras, irreducible representations, character theory, Schur's lemma, orthogonality relations, induced representations, and tensor products of representations.

Reference: Linear Representations of Finite Groups by Jean-Pierre Serre & lectures by Prof. D. S. Nagaraj.
